# Exploratory global-domain certificate probe

This probe uses normalized leaf inputs with the known domain bound
`||x_leaf|| <= 1`, so the global certificate is constructed independently of
the held-out batch. It compares `global_certificate_optimal` with the
historical `pathwise_global` heuristic on two topologies, ten seeds, six
budgets, and 300 held-out samples per trial.

The raw artifact is `global_certificate_probe_2026-08-09.json`, generated by
`experiments/run_global_certificate_probe.py`. Results remain exploratory and
do not alter the preregistered Level 1 campaign.

Observed artifact summary:

| Topology | Pairs | Mean held-out RMS reduction | Candidate better fraction | Certificate holds on held-out |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| chain depth 3 | 60 | 0.0148 | 0.6667 | 1.0000 |
| balanced binary, 4 leaves | 60 | 0.0135 | 0.6667 | 1.0000 |

The certificate is conservative but domain-valid for the normalized leaf
inputs in this probe; no held-out sample violated it.
