\section{Projection layer}
\texttt{seion\_kgr/kernels.py::StiefelProjector}: $Q$ on the Stiefel manifold via QR retraction
of a learned parameter, $P = QQ^*$. \textbf{Status: \ProvedU} that $Q^*Q=I$ holds by construction
of QR (any $R$), verified after real gradient steps (isometry/idempotent residuals
$\sim10^{-7}$, not just at init) --- this caught a real bug during the base engineering work: an
earlier sign-disambiguation step (\texttt{torch.sign(...).clamp\_min(1e-12)}) silently replaced
negative signs with $10^{-12}$ and shrank that column to near zero, breaking orthonormality after
training; fixed by removing the unneeded correction (QR's $Q$ is orthonormal regardless of sign
convention).

\texttt{seion\_kgr/projection.py::measure\_closure\_leakage\_sample} computes the sample-mean
closure-leakage ratio --- explicitly labeled an \texttt{empirical\_error\_predictor}, never a
certified bound (\S07).

\section{Rank allocation policies}
Six policies in \texttt{seion\_kgr/rank\_controller.py}: \texttt{uniform},
\texttt{singular\_energy}, \texttt{gradient\_sensitivity}, \texttt{pathwise},
\texttt{local\_error\_greedy}, \texttt{hybrid} (the recommended default, a weighted combination of
the other four's signals), plus \texttt{random} as a control. \textbf{Status: \DefC}, all six
unit-tested for budget/max-rank compliance; \texttt{hybrid} verified structurally distinct from
\texttt{pathwise} alone (never defaults to the single signal the confirmatory
\texttt{adaptive\_tensor\_network} evidence already showed loses to \texttt{uniform} and
\texttt{local\_error\_greedy} at equal budget --- assumption A12).

\texttt{compare\_policies} runs every policy through a caller-supplied objective and reports
regret against the best-tried (not a proven oracle) --- unit-tested with a hand-computed objective,
not run against a real trained model's rank-allocation decision in this campaign.

\section{Instrumentation wired into training}
\texttt{seion\_kgr/train.py}'s \texttt{\_module\_diagnostics} computes, per epoch (when the
relevant branch is enabled): closure leakage (path reasoner only, if its projector is enabled),
singular-energy-uncaptured (via exact SVD of the CP law's output weight matrix), and
gradient-sensitivity (last-batch gradient norm on that module's parameters, an explicit
simplification stated in code comments). These feed \texttt{rank\_history.jsonl} together with a
\texttt{compare\_policies} call each eval epoch --- real, executed, verified end to end on real
WN18RR data (\texttt{campaigns/gate12/audit\_smoke\_run\_selector} class of runs during Phase B
development; superseded by Tier~0 for this package's canonical evidence).

\section{What is NOT done: a Pareto study}
\label{sec:pareto}
\textbf{No MRR-vs-rank/VRAM/latency Pareto curve was generated on benchmark data in this
campaign.} The instrumentation to produce one exists and is tested (above), but running it across
a rank sweep on FB15K-237/WN18RR at meaningful scale requires the same path-reasoner-scaling
resolution needed for a real confirmatory campaign (\S09) --- not attempted here, given the
session's compute budget was spent on engineering closure and a bounded 2-ladder-point screening
pass instead, per the mandate's own priority order (item 7, "projection/rank-controller Pareto
study", explicitly ranked below primary screening and confirmatory work).

\textbf{Status: OPEN.} This is the single most concrete, cheaply-scoped follow-up: given the
instrumentation already exists, a rank sweep at fixed budget on the Tier~0 A3 configuration
(TuckER + path + seion, subsampled data) would produce a real, if still screening-tier, Pareto
curve within a session of similar length to this one.
