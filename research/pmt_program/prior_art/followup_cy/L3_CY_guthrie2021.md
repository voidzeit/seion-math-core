## Abstract-level content (paraphrased)

Closed-form **outer approximations** of Minkowski sums and products of sets in the complex plane. In polar coordinates, a pair of
contours bounds the radial distance at each angle. The problem is rewritten as a sum-of-squares program, and numerical examples are given.
