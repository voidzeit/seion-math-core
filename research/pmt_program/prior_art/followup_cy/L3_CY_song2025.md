## Results (paraphrased)

- **Prop. 3.3.** Restates the two-factor Ogura–Yamada bound for the modulus $k(T_1T_2)$, including degenerate cases.
- **Prop. 3.6 / Cor. 3.7 (m factors).** If $T_1$ is not surjective, or $T_m$ is not injective, etc., then
  $k(T_1\cdots T_m)\ge1/2$. In particular a product of $m$ projections (the first or last onto a proper set) has modulus
  at least $1/2$. This is a **lower** bound from non-bijectivity, not an evaluation of the CY constant.
- **Cor. 3.9.** Products of orthogonal matrices $\ne\mathrm{Id}$ have modulus 1.
- §7 extends the Bauschke–Bendit–Moursi two-projection formula from $\mathbb R^2$ to Hilbert spaces ($m=2$).
