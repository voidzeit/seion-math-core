# P4 — k=3 topology sharpness status

For the gated planar-rotation family, the reproduced exact forms are:

```text
chain:     E_proj(eta) = 3 eta^2 sqrt(1-eta^2), best ratio 3/4
branching: E_proj(eta) =     eta^2 sqrt(1-eta^2), best ratio 1/4
```

Both optimize at `eta = 1/sqrt(2)`. Topology dependence is therefore exact
inside this restricted family: the chain construction is three times larger
than branching at the shared optimum.

The general k=3 class remains open. These formulas do not prove that either
topology is globally extremal, nor that no other topology, dimension, rank,
field, or independent-law family can approach the universal coefficient two.
