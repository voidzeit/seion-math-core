\section*{Exact $k=2$ constant for a contractive gated-planar repeated law}
\label{sec:m17-gated-contraction}

Let $P$ be an orthogonal projector, choose unit vectors
$e_0\in\operatorname{Ran}P$ and $e_1\in\ker P$, and put $Q=I-P$.  Consider
the repeated gated law
\[
  \mu_A(x,y)=A x\,\langle e_0,y\rangle,
\]
where $A$ maps the active plane
$\operatorname{span}\{e_0,e_1\}$ into itself, $\|A\|\le M$, and
$\|QAP\|\le\rho=\eta M$.  Use unit leaves $e_0$ at the binary chain's
three leaves.  Define $C_{2,\mathrm{gate\text{-}contr}}^P(\eta)$ as the
supremum of the projected error normalized by $\rho M$ over this class.

\textbf{Theorem.}
\[
  \boxed{C_{2,\mathrm{gate\text{-}contr}}^P(\eta)=1
  \qquad(0<\eta\le1).}
\]

\textbf{Proof.}  The ambient value is $A^2e_0$ and the recursively
projected value is $PAPAe_0$.  Hence the projected-root difference is the
exact identity
\[
  P A^2e_0-PAPAe_0=PAQ A e_0.
\]
Because $e_0\in\operatorname{Ran}P$,
$\|Q A e_0\|\le\|QAP\|\le\rho$, while
$\|PAQ\|\le\|A\|\le M$.  Therefore
$E_{\mathrm{proj}}\le M\rho$.

For sharpness, on the active plane take
\[
  A=\begin{pmatrix}0&M\\\rho&0\end{pmatrix}
\]
and let $A$ vanish on the orthogonal complement.  Since $0<\rho\le M$,
$\|A\|=M$ and $\|QAP\|=\rho$.  Moreover
$PAQ A e_0=M\rho e_0$, so the upper bound is attained.  The gate has norm
one, hence $\|\mu_A\|=\|A\|$, and the same law is used at both nodes.
\hfill$\square$

The canonical orthogonal gated rotation is the special case
$A=M R_\theta$ with $\sin\theta=\eta$; it has the narrower value
$E_{\mathrm{proj}}=\eta^2M^2$.  The theorem shows that orthogonality of the
active operator, rather than law sharing itself, is what prevents saturation
for that narrower construction.

\textbf{Boundary.}  This closes the explicitly defined contractive gated-planar
subclass.  It does not claim a result for every possible meaning of
``broader gated-planar'', for variable gates, arbitrary leaf directions, or
non-planar shared laws.
