\section*{M22: Rank-one/common-leaf same-law chain at high $\eta$}
\label{sec:m22-rank-one-high-eta}

Consider the strict binary $k=3$ chain class with a single repeated bilinear
law, a common unit projected leaf $e_0$, and the rank-one projector
$P=e_0e_0^*$.  The independent-law M14 theorem supplies the upper bound
$C_{3,\mathrm{same},r=1}^P(\eta)\le W_3(\eta)$ because this class is a
subclass of the independent-law class.

For $\eta\ge\alpha:=\sqrt{2/3}$, use one repeated gated rotation law
\[
 \mu(x,y)=A x\langle e_0,y\rangle,
 \qquad
 A=\begin{pmatrix}1/\sqrt3&-\sqrt{2/3}\\
                    \sqrt{2/3}&1/\sqrt3\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Its norm is one and its projected-input closure defect is $\alpha\le\eta$.
The ambient chain output is $A^3e_0$, while the recursively projected output
is $P A P A P A e_0$.  Therefore
\[
 \|P A^3e_0-P A P A P A e_0\|
 =3\alpha^2/\sqrt3=2/\sqrt3.
\]
After normalization by $\eta$, this is
\[
 \frac{2}{\sqrt3\,\eta}=W_3(\eta),
 \qquad \alpha\le\eta\le1.
\]

\textbf{Theorem.} In the rank-one/common-leaf same-law chain class,
\[
 \boxed{C_{3,\mathrm{same},r=1}^P(\eta)=W_3(\eta)}
 \qquad\text{for }\sqrt{2/3}\le\eta\le1.
\]
The low-defect regime $0<\eta<\sqrt{2/3}$ remains open for this strict
class; branching, gated variants beyond this rotation family, and $k\ge4$
fixed-$\eta$ sharpness remain separate questions.
\hfill$\square$
