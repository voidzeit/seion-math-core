\section*{M21: Tagged same-law fixed-$\eta$ constants for binary $k=3$}
\label{sec:m21-tagged-same-law}

The phrase ``same law'' does not by itself require a rank-one projector or
identical leaf directions.  Let $\mathcal S_{3,\mathrm{tag}}$ be the finite
dimensional real binary $k=3$ class with one repeated bilinear law, one fixed
coordinate projector, and arbitrary unit projected leaf vectors.  Projected
leaf directions may contain finitely many orthogonal tags.

\textbf{Lemma (tagged direct-sum simulation).}  Given the M14 chain witness,
there is one bilinear law on a finite orthogonal direct sum whose three
occurrences act respectively as the M14 bottom, middle, and root laws when
their leaf-slot tags select the corresponding block.  The blocks have
orthogonal input supports, so the shared law has operator norm at most one.
On projected inputs, the only nonzero normal output is the bottom leakage,
whose norm is $\eta$; the middle leakage is also at most $\eta$ and the root
output is projected.  The projected and ambient evaluations therefore agree
with the M14 witness exactly.  The same construction with two bottom tags and
one bilinear root block simulates M15.

The direct-sum norm estimate is
\[
 \left\|\sum_j B_j(x_j,y_j)\right\|^2
 \le \max_j\|B_j\|_{\mathrm{op}}^2
       \sum_j\|x_j\|^2\|y_j\|^2
 \le \max_j\|B_j\|_{\mathrm{op}}^2\|x\|^2\|y\|^2.
\]

\textbf{Theorem.} For the explicitly declared tagged same-law class,
\[
 \boxed{
 C_{3,\mathrm{tag\text{-}same}}^P(\eta)=W_3(\eta),
 \qquad 0<\eta\le1,
 }
\]
for both the ordered chain and ordered branching topology. The upper bound
is inherited from the independent-law class M14/M15, and the tagged
direct-sum witnesses give equality.

This theorem removes law sharing as an obstruction when finite projected tags
are admissible. It does not close the stricter rank-one/common-leaf same-law
class, nor gated-planar repeated-law subclasses, variable-gate variants, or
fixed-$\eta$ independent-law trees with $k\ge4$.
\hfill$\square$
