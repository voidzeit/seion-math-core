\section*{Exact formula for every ordered full-binary gated-rotation tree}
\label{sec:gated-rotation-full-binary}

Let $T$ be a finite ordered full-binary tree.  Use the same homogeneous
gated-planar rotation law at every internal node, unit leaves equal to $e_0$,
and a coordinate orthogonal projector whose range contains $e_0$ and whose
complement contains the active coordinate $e_r$.  Write
\[
  c=\cos\theta=\sqrt{1-\eta^2},
  \qquad \eta=\sin\theta,
\]
and let $k(T)$ be the number of internal nodes.  Define recursively
\[
  d(\text{leaf})=0, \qquad
  d((A,B))=1+d(A),
\]
and
\[
  a(\text{leaf})=1, \qquad
  a((A,B))=a(A)a(B)\cos(d(B)\theta).
\]
Then, in the $M=L_T=1$ normalization,
\[
  \boxed{
  E_T^{\mathrm{proj}}(\eta)=
  \left|a(T)\cos(d(T)\theta)-c^{k(T)}\right|.
  }
\]
For a law scaled by $M$, the right-hand side is multiplied by $M^{k(T)}$.

\textbf{Proof.}  Let $z_T$ denote the ambient value.  The gated law is
\[
  \mu(x,y)=R_\theta x\,\langle e_0,y\rangle,
\]
where $R_\theta$ is the active planar rotation.  Induction gives
\[
  z_T=a(T)R_\theta^{d(T)}e_0.
\]
Indeed, if $z_A=a(A)R_\theta^{d(A)}e_0$ and
$z_B=a(B)R_\theta^{d(B)}e_0$, then
\[
  z_{(A,B)}=a(A)a(B)\cos(d(B)\theta)
  R_\theta^{d(A)+1}e_0.
\]
The recursively projected value has the form $r_T=b(T)e_0$.  Since
$P R_\theta e_0=c e_0$ and both projected child gates equal their $e_0$
coordinates, induction gives
\[
  b(\text{leaf})=1, \qquad b((A,B))=c\,b(A)b(B),
  \qquad b(T)=c^{k(T)}.
\]
Finally $Pz_T=a(T)\cos(d(T)\theta)e_0$, which proves the formula.
The argument uses only the active two-plane and is therefore independent of
the ambient dimension and projector rank, subject to the stated coordinate
conditions.  The exact repository evaluator
`gated_rotation_full_binary.py` verifies every ordered shape through four
internal nodes at several dimension/rank pairs.  $\square$

\textbf{Special cases.}  For the left comb, $a(T)=1$ and the formula reduces
to $|T_k(c)-c^k|$.  For the three-node branching tree,
$a(T)=c$ and $d(T)=2$, giving $\eta^2c$.  Thus the earlier closed forms are
instances of one topology-wide recurrence.

\textbf{Scope boundary.}  This closes the declared homogeneous gated-planar
rotation family for every finite ordered full-binary topology.  It does not
determine the global extremal constants for independent or arbitrary laws,
nor does it prove the universal $(k-1)$ coefficient sharp for that larger
class.
