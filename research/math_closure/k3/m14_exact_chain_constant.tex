\section*{M14: Exact fixed-$\eta$ constant for the independent-law $k=3$ chain}
\label{sec:m14-exact-chain}

Let $C_{3,\mathrm{ind}}^{P,\mathrm{chain}}(\eta)$ be the projected-root
constant for the ordered binary chain, normalized by $\rho M^2L_T$, with
independently chosen real bilinear laws and orthogonal projectors. M13 gives
the unconditional upper bound
\[
 C_{3,\mathrm{ind}}^{P,\mathrm{chain}}(\eta)\le W_3(\eta),
\]
where
\[
 W_3(\eta)=
 \begin{cases}
 \sqrt{4-3\eta^2},&0<\eta\le\sqrt{2/3},\\[1mm]
 \dfrac{2}{\sqrt3\,\eta},&\sqrt{2/3}\le\eta\le1.
 \end{cases}
\tag{M14.1}
\]

\subsection*{Theorem}

For every $0<\eta\le1$,
\[
 \boxed{C_{3,\mathrm{ind}}^{P,\mathrm{chain}}(\eta)=W_3(\eta).}
\tag{M14.2}
\]
The supremum is attained by a two-dimensional real construction with a
rank-one coordinate projector. The construction uses distinct laws at the
three internal nodes, so it belongs to the independent-law class.

\subsection*{Explicit saturating construction}

It is enough to take $M=1$, $L_T=1$, $\rho=\eta$, and
$P=e_0e_0^*$. Set
\[
 t:=\min\{\eta,\sqrt{2/3}\},\qquad
 p:=\sqrt{1-t^2},\qquad c:=\sqrt{1-t^2}.
\]
At the first node use
\[
 \mu_1(x,y)=\langle x,e_0\rangle\langle y,e_0\rangle
 (t e_1+p e_0).
\]
Its operator norm is one and its closure residual has norm $t\le\eta$.
At the second node let $N_t$ be the orthogonal map
\[
 N_t e_0=t e_1-c e_0,\qquad
 N_t e_1=c e_1+t e_0,
\]
and set $\mu_2(x,y)=\langle y,e_0\rangle N_t x$. Its operator norm is one,
while its closure residual on the projected input space has norm $t\le\eta$.

Let
\[
 x:=N_t e_1=c e_1+t e_0,\qquad
 z:=e_1,qquad
 v:=t x+p t z.
\]
Define the root linear map $A$ by
\[
 A h=\frac{\langle v,h\rangle}{\|v\|}e_0,
 \qquad \mu_3(x,y)=\langle y,e_0\rangle A x.
\]
Then $\|A\|=\|\mu_3\|_{\rm op}=1$ and the root closure residual is zero.
For unit leaves $e_0$, the first-node ambient/reduced split is
\[
 D_1=t e_1,\qquad R_1=p e_0,
\]
and the exact second-node error split is
\[
 F_2-R_2=tN_t e_1+p(I-P)N_t e_0=t x+p t z=v.
\]
Consequently the projected root error is $\|v\|$:
\[
 E_T^P=\sqrt{t^2+p^2t^2+2pt^2c}.
\tag{M14.3}
\]
For $t=\eta\le\sqrt{2/3}$, division by $\eta$ gives
$\sqrt{4-3\eta^2}$. For $t=\sqrt{2/3}$, it gives
$2/(\sqrt3\,\eta)$. Thus the construction attains $W_3(\eta)$ in both
regimes, and M13 supplies the matching upper bound. \hfill$\square$

\subsection*{Boundary}

This closes the exact fixed-$\eta$ constant only for the independent-law
ordered chain. The branching constant, a common branching envelope, the
same-law/restricted gated subclasses, and the arbitrary-tree $(k-1)$
sharpness conjecture remain open. The result is internal and remains subject
to independent human review and theorem-level novelty review.
