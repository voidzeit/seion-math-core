\section*{Exact formula for arity-compatible gated-rotation trees}
\label{sec:gated-rotation-general-arity}

The binary theorem extends to finite ordered rooted trees with variable
arity.  At an internal node of arity $m\ge2$, use the arity-compatible law
\[
  \mu_m(x_1,\ldots,x_m)
  =R_\theta x_1\prod_{j=2}^m\langle e_0,x_j\rangle,
\]
with the same active planar rotation $R_\theta$ for every $m$.  Let all
leaves be $e_0$, and let the coordinate projector contain $e_0$ while its
complement contains the active normal coordinate.

For an ordered rooted tree $T$, define
\[
 d(\text{leaf})=0,\qquad d((T_1,\ldots,T_m))=1+d(T_1),
\]
and
\[
 a(\text{leaf})=1,\qquad
 a((T_1,\ldots,T_m))
 =a(T_1)\prod_{j=2}^m a(T_j)\cos(d(T_j)\theta).
\]
If $k(T)$ is the number of internal nodes and
$c=\cos\theta=\sqrt{1-\eta^2}$, then
\[
  \boxed{
  E_T^{\mathrm{proj}}(\eta)=
  \left|a(T)\cos(d(T)\theta)-c^{k(T)}\right|
  }
\]
in the $M=L_T=1$ normalization.  A common law scale $M$ multiplies the
right-hand side by $M^{k(T)}$.

\textbf{Proof.}  Inductively write the ambient value as
$z_T=a(T)R_\theta^{d(T)}e_0$.  At a node, each secondary child contributes
the scalar
$a(T_j)\cos(d(T_j)\theta)$ to its gate, while the first child contributes
one additional rotation.  This gives exactly the displayed recurrence for
$a$ and $d$.  For projected evaluation write $r_T=b(T)e_0$.  Since
$P R_\theta e_0=c e_0$, every internal node gives
$b(T)=c\prod_j b(T_j)$.  Induction therefore yields
$b(T)=c^{k(T)}$.  Projecting the ambient root gives
$Pz_T=a(T)\cos(d(T)\theta)e_0$, proving the formula. $\square$

The exact repository evaluator `gated_rotation_general_arity.py` checks all
ordered shapes with up to three internal nodes and arities two and three at
four dimension/rank pairs.  This theorem is restricted to the shared
arity-compatible gated law and unit $e_0$ leaves; it does not establish the
global extremal constants for independent or arbitrary multilinear laws.
