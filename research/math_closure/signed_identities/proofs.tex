\section*{M4: Signed combinations and structural identities}

\subsection*{Exact algebraic results}

\textbf{Level-1 (syntactic) cancellation.} For all 5 named identities,
\texttt{combined\_terms()} (exact syntactic tree-shape collapse) returns
the same number of terms as the input — confirmed by direct inspection —
so none of these identities have accidentally-repeated syntactic subtrees
to cancel before applying the triangle inequality. Any improvement over
the plain triangle bound must come from level 2 (shared-subtree DAG,
not attempted) or level 3 (adversarial search over general laws, or an
exact algebraic argument as in M1/M2/M3).

\textbf{The declared 6-term GJI variant} is resolved in full in
\texttt{research/math\_closure/gji/} (M1): \textsc{disproved\_by\_counterexample}
for the general claim (exact rational counterexample, nonzero), and
\textsc{proved} for the collinear-leaves sub-case — which is exactly the
regime the adversarial search (constants\_table.csv row
\texttt{named\_gji\_variants}) tested, fully explaining its
near-machine-zero reading without supporting a general identity.

\textbf{A structural curiosity found this session}: under the
\emph{different}, structured gated-planar-rotation law used for the
k=2/k=3 exact results (not the fully generic random tensors the
adversarial search below uses), the binary Jacobiator's triangle
discrepancy is exactly $0$ for every $\eta\in[0,1]$ (verified by exact
symbolic substitution through the repo's own evaluator). This is a fact
about that specific structured law, not evidence toward or against the
general extremal question in \S\ref{sec:extremal} — the two searches use
different admissible sub-classes and must not be conflated (see
\texttt{conventions.md}).

\subsection*{Proved universal upper bound}
\label{sec:extremal}

Every identity's triangle-inequality bound
$\sum_\alpha|c_\alpha|(k_\alpha-1)$ is the mission's proved universal
constant, unconditional (no counterexample found in 4,000 adversarial
trials per identity, nor in this session's exact work).
