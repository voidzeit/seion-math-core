\section{Exact path constant for the nonnegative first-order DAG channel class}
\label{sec:exact-dag-path-constant}

The multilinear telescoping recurrence produces a nonnegative first-order
envelope.  Fix a finite acyclic DAG, nonnegative edge gains $g_e$, and local
source bounds $0\le s_v\le\bar s_v$.  The associated channel class is
\begin{equation*}
 d_v=s_v+\sum_{e:u\to v}g_e d_u,
 \qquad d_{\mathrm{root}}\text{ the certified output error}.
\end{equation*}
Parallel edges are retained and represent repeated input slots.  Let $w_v$ be
the total gain of all directed paths from $v$ to the root, with the empty path
at the root having weight one.  Reverse topological propagation gives
\begin{equation*}
 w_{\mathrm{root}}=1,
 \qquad
 w_u=\sum_{e:u\to v}g_e w_v.
\end{equation*}
Substitution, or induction in topological order, yields the identity
\begin{equation*}
 d_{\mathrm{root}}=\sum_v w_v s_v.
\end{equation*}
Because every coefficient is nonnegative, the exact supremum over the declared
box of source amplitudes is therefore
\begin{equation*}
 C_{\mathrm{channel}}(G,g,\bar s)
   =\sum_v w_v\bar s_v.
\end{equation*}
It is attained by the admissible realization $s_v=\bar s_v$ at every source.
Thus the path constant is sharp for the nonnegative channel class, including
shared subexpressions and repeated slots.  When the gains are instantiated by
multilinear operator and value enclosures, this is an exact sharpness result
for the first-order envelope, not automatically for the original multilinear
operator class: simultaneous attainment of all operator and normal-residual
equalities remains a separate compatibility question.
