# Author response to adversarial reviews

We accept the central recommendation: the v2 foundations manuscript is a
rigorous draft and not a submission-ready novelty paper. The exact reduction,
identity inheritance, and spectral snapping results are now labelled as
standard or supporting results, and the approximate tree recurrence is
labelled `PROVED_AUXILIARY` with no novelty claim.

We also accept the numerical qualifications. The 180-row matrix is evidence
for the finite registered instances only. The v2 index is separate from the
legacy history, repeated legacy executions are not counted as independent,
and the no-gap construction is retained as a negative control. Degenerate
reference comparisons now report `NA_zero_variance` rather than a fabricated
effect size.

The remaining blockers are explicit: a genuinely new theorem-level result has
not been established, no sharpness/lower-bound theorem is claimed, and the
author metadata still requires a verified email and ORCID before submission.
Until those conditions change, the correct release state is draft/not for
submission plus a reproducibility/software companion.
