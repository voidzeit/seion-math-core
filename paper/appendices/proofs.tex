\section{Proof details}

The curvature expansion follows by writing the four-term expression
\[
[L_x,L_y]z-L_{[x,y]}z=x\circ(y\circ z)-y\circ(x\circ z)-(x\circ y)\circ z+(y\circ x)\circ z
\]
and grouping the terms as $A(y,x,z)-A(x,y,z)$. The cohomology descent proof is the cycle/boundary preservation argument stated in Section 13.
