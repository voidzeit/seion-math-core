\section{Reproducibility}

Canonical commands:

\begin{verbatim}
.\scripts\run_full_blackwell.ps1
.\scripts\build_paper.ps1
\end{verbatim}

Run manifests record commit, OS, CPU/GPU metadata, Python, seeds, tolerances, artifact paths, and hashes. The final PDF hash is recorded in the release checksum file.

The machine-readable claim registry is maintained in 
\texttt{claims/claims\_registry.yaml}; generated evidence matrices distinguish definitions, proved statements, conditional theorems, numerical observations, empirical heuristics, conjectures, open problems, and refutations.
