\section{Counterexamples and falsification}

Two negative controls are central. First, threshold spectral snapping can change rank under arbitrarily small perturbations when an eigenvalue is at the threshold. Second, standard left-action curvature is generally a difference of two ordered associators, not one raw associator. The repository registers both so a broad statement fails visibly rather than being silently absorbed into a score.
