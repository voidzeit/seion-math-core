\section{Structure-preserving projector reduction}

For $P=QQ^*$ with $Q^*Q=I$, the finite closure diagnostic is
\[
E_{\mathrm{closure}}=\mathbb E\frac{\|(I-P)\mu(PX_1,\ldots,PX_n)\|^2}{\|\mu(PX_1,\ldots,PX_n)\|^2+\varepsilon}.
\]
The reduced law is $\overline\mu(z_1,\ldots,z_n)=Q^*\mu(Qz_1,\ldots,Qz_n)$. The certificate compares a known invariant projector, random and PCA controls, and an empirical closure-minimizing projector.
