\section{Truncated cohomological operators}

Let $d^2=0$ on a finite cochain complex. If a same-degree operator $T$ commutes with $d$, then it preserves cycles and boundaries and descends to cohomology. This is a finite quotient argument, not a pseudodifferential or microlocal upgrade. The package also computes finite Hodge Laplacians, harmonic bases, and commutator defects.
