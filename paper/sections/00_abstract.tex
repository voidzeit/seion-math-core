\begin{abstract}
We define a finite-dimensional verification environment for Kernel-Integrated N-Ary Laws. The executable nucleus contains typed n-linear maps, dense and CP representations, separately named ternary composition conventions, associator and symmetry defects, structure-preserving projectors, finite kernel models, operator constructions, and truncated cohomological compatibility checks. The paper reports only finite algebraic proofs and declared numerical observations. It does not claim a universal curvature theorem, a continuum limit, a physical field theory, or an application result. Every numerical quantity is associated with a seed, precision, convention, and artifact path.
\end{abstract}
