\section{Numerical methods and precision}

Critical quantities carry a precision tier, input scaling, sample count, seed, and a condition indicator. The local ladder includes float32, float64, complex64, and complex128; arbitrary precision and exact symbolic checks are separate tracks. A residual near machine precision is not accepted as meaningful without scale or conditioning metadata.
