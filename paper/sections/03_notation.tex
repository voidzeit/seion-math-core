\section{Notation and epistemic statuses}

The canonical object is
\[
\mathfrak S=(V,\mu_n,K,A_\mu,\mathcal E,P,\overline\mu).
\]
Here $V$ may be a typed family of finite-dimensional spaces, $\mu_n$ is an $n$-linear law, $K$ is its coordinate tensor, $A_\mu$ is a selected composition defect, $\mathcal E$ is a declared functional, $P$ is a candidate projector, and $\overline\mu$ is the reduced law. A status such as \emph{numerically verified} is never substituted for \emph{proved}.
