\section{Limitations}

The initial release is finite-dimensional and mostly NumPy-based. The empirical projector search is not globally convergent. The CP approximation is a deterministic greedy approximation and does not establish uniqueness. The multiscale curves are finite-sequence observations. Continuous kernel limits, strong operator convergence, microlocal regularity, D-modules, Riemann--Hilbert, and algebraicity are not combined into a theorem. No application adapter is evidence for the mathematical core.
