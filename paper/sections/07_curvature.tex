\section{Curvature conventions}

We distinguish constitutive algebraic curvature, defined by selecting $R_{\mathrm{alg}}:=A_\mu$, from operator-induced curvature. For a bilinear product,
\[
L_xz=x\circ z,\qquad R_{\mathrm{standard}}(x,y)=[L_x,L_y]-L_{[x,y]},\qquad [x,y]=x\circ y-y\circ x.
\]
The finite identity proved in the repository is
\[
R_{\mathrm{standard}}(x,y)z=A(y,x,z)-A(x,y,z).
\]
Thus a raw associator equality requires an additional hypothesis or a different declared convention. The curvature table is generated from the registry.
