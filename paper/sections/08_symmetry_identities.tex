\section{Symmetry and coherence identities}

Cyclic symmetry, full permutation symmetry, antisymmetry, Jacobiators, Akivis residuals, generalized Jacobi variants, and the Filippov fundamental identity are separate evaluators. For example, the ternary FI residual used by the package is
\[
\mu(x_1,x_2,\mu(y_1,y_2,y_3))-\sum_{j=1}^3\mu(y_1,\ldots,\mu(x_1,x_2,y_j),\ldots,y_3).
\]
Its formula and signs are recorded in code. A low residual does not identify one identity with another.
