\section{Multiscale and interscale structure}

The repository defines finite families $(V_N,\mu_N,P_N)$ and explicit prolongation/restriction maps. It records law transport, projector transport, principal angles, factor alignment, and finite-sequence slopes. Four resolutions are used in the registered illustrative suite. A sequence of four finite values is not presented as a continuum-limit proof: a topology, uniform estimates, and compactness or convergence arguments remain required.
