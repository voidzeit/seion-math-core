\section{Conclusion}

The repository implements a small but explicit mathematical nucleus: typed n-ary laws, distinct ternary compositions, algebraic and operator conventions, projector reduction, finite kernels, finite cohomology, and a reproducible evidence contract. The strongest conclusions are definitions and finite algebraic proofs. The numerical experiments are useful for falsification and comparison within their declared scope, but they do not establish universal laws or continuum theories.
