\section{Introduction}

The central question is whether an internal n-ary law can support a coherent algebraic and computational chain. We make this question testable by fixing typed finite-dimensional objects before discussing limits or interpretation. The chain studied here is
\[
V\longrightarrow \mu_n\longrightarrow A_\mu\longrightarrow \mathcal E\longrightarrow P\longrightarrow \overline\mu,
\]
with multiscale, operator, and cohomological extensions kept conditional or finite.

The contribution is an executable specification and evidence discipline. It is not a claim that the vocabulary is new: associators, tensor decompositions, projectors, and finite cochain complexes are established constructions. The repository combines them under a common contract that records definitions, assumptions, proofs, counterexamples, and observations separately.
