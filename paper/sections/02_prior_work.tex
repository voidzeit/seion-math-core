\section{Prior work and positioning}

The framework touches non-associative algebra, ternary and Filippov algebras, operadic compositions, CP tensor decompositions, invariant-subspace reduction, kernel operators, and discrete Hodge theory. The repository's novelty registry avoids renaming a known construction as a new theorem. Its limited new component is organizational: a declared law, a declared defect convention, a reduction diagnostic, and a reproducible artifact are joined in one traceable path.

The prior-art matrix is generated from \texttt{claims/novelty\_registry.yaml}. No priority claim is made for the classical identities or for CP gauge non-uniqueness.
