# Projectors and reduced laws

For an orthonormal basis `Q`, `P=QQ*`. The finite closure leakage is the sample mean of

\[
\frac{\|(I-P)\mu(Px_1,\ldots,Px_n)\|^2}{\|\mu(Px_1,\ldots,Px_n)\|^2+\varepsilon}.
\]

The reduced law is `mu_bar(z_1,...,z_n)=Q*mu(Qz_1,...,Qz_n)`. A low value on a finite sample is recorded as a numerical observation only.
