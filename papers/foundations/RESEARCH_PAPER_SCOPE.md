# Mathematical research paper scope

This workstream is the proposed research-paper nucleus:

**Structure-Preserving Reduction of Finite-Dimensional N-Ary Laws: Exact
Functoriality, Associator Stability, and Projection Error Bounds**

The central theorem program is registered in
`governance/RESEARCH_SOFTWARE_SPLIT.yaml` and must be proved, weakened to a
conditional theorem, or marked conjectural before it is presented as a paper
contribution. The current `paper/main.tex` is a broad finite-dimensional
release note; this scope file does not claim that the stronger program has
already been established.

Required evidence for the research track:

- exact invariant-subspace reduction with explicit maps and hypotheses;
- preservation of operadic partial compositions and polynomial identities;
- explicit approximate-closure constants;
- spectral snapping stability under a gap and a no-gap counterexample;
- prior-art comparison that distinguishes known constructions from new results;
- experiments that test the bounds rather than decorate the exposition.
