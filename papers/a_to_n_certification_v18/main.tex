\begin{abstract}
We replace a 14-block legacy audit script's averaging-based scoring system
(WARN scores 0.5, N/A scores 1.0, no FAIL state exists) with a fail-closed
typed-gate taxonomy: ten states from \textsc{structural\_identity\_pass}
through \textsc{exact\_certificate}, combined by minimum across eight
critical gates, never by average. Applying it to the legacy evidence and
to a from-scratch v18 reimplementation, we find: every one of 19 unique
historical runs is capped at \textsc{empirical\_screening\_pass} (all
\texttt{eval\_mode=screening}, including runs named \texttt{*\_CERT\_*});
the ``coherent dynamic curvature'' commutator explanation (block B)
performs \emph{worse than the trivial zero predictor} in all 15 real
checkpoints with block-B data, and a controlled ablation matrix identifies
the mechanism as genuine conflict with an associator/GJI-family objective,
not insufficient capacity, gradient starvation, or scale imbalance alone;
an independently-trained three-resolution interscale transport experiment
(blocks E, J, M) finds no persistence signal beyond a single unexplained
per-mode anomaly; and a methodological error caught by our own required
negative control --- comparing subspace bases via free-unitary Procrustes,
which is vacuous because the unitary group acts transitively on
same-size orthonormal frames --- is documented and fixed with principal
angles. No block in this pass reaches a state stronger than
\textsc{empirical\_screening\_pass} or \textsc{statistically\_validated\_pass}
for any scientific (as opposed to purely algebraic/structural) claim, and
no process here self-issues a final certification: the ceiling is
\textsc{pass\_a\_to\_n\_partial\_certification}, pending human review.
\end{abstract}

\section{Why the legacy scoring system cannot be trusted}
\label{sec:legacy}

The legacy script (\texttt{seion\_master\_audit\_A\_to\_N\_v17\_blackwell\_repro\_fix.py},
2653 lines, hashed and archived at
\texttt{spectral/legacy/v17/}) assigns each of 14 blocks one of
$\{\textsc{pass}, \textsc{pass\_strong}, \textsc{warn}, \textsc{warn\_geometric}, \textsc{n/a}\}$
and computes \texttt{master\_score} as $100\times$ the mean of per-block
scores, where \textsc{warn}$\mapsto 0.5$ and \textsc{n/a}$\mapsto 1.0$.
There is no \textsc{fail} state anywhere in the vocabulary. Consequently a
block that never even ran (no high-resolution scale configured) scores
full credit, and a block that failed outright scores half credit, not
zero.

We ingested all 19 unique historical runs (9 logged in
\texttt{REPRO\_SUMMARIES\_A\_TO\_M\_VALIDATED.txt}, 18 on-disk run
directories, 8 overlapping) and reconstructed their resume lineage. All 9
logged runs share \texttt{seed=3} and are one continuous non-strict-resume
chain (\texttt{restore\_rng=false}, \texttt{strict\_resume=false}
throughout) rooted at a single bootstrap --- not nine independent trials.
Every run, without exception, has \texttt{eval\_mode="screening"},
including every directory named \texttt{*\_CERT\_*}. Two distinct
\texttt{script\_sha256} values appear across the lineage: the script
currently in the repository matches 8 of 9 logged runs but not the
earliest bootstrap run, meaning the script changed mid-campaign without
this being tracked anywhere in the legacy artifacts.

\section{The typed-gate taxonomy}
\label{sec:taxonomy}

We define ten typed states (full definitions and non-implications in
\texttt{spectral/certification\_v18/GATE\_TAXONOMY.md}):
\textsc{structural\_identity\_pass}, \textsc{numerical\_sanity\_pass},
\textsc{empirical\_screening\_pass}, \textsc{statistically\_validated\_pass},
\textsc{validated\_numerical\_certificate}, \textsc{exact\_certificate},
\textsc{warn}, \textsc{fail}, \textsc{not\_applicable}, and
\textsc{not\_certifiable\_as\_defined}. These are not a numeric scale.
Eight critical gates (\texttt{projector\_gate}, \texttt{algebra\_gate},
\texttt{dynamic\_explanation\_gate}, \texttt{interscale\_gate},
\texttt{gauge\_gate}, \texttt{persistence\_gate},
\texttt{reproducibility\_gate}, \texttt{mathematical\_proof\_gate}) each
take the \emph{minimum} (weakest, never averaged) status over their
contributing blocks. A code-level enforcement point
(\texttt{gates.py:assign\_block\_status}) raises
\texttt{ScreeningCertificateViolation} if a screening-mode run is ever
assigned a certificate-tier status --- a hard error, not a silent
downgrade. No code path in this suite can emit
\texttt{PASS\_A\_TO\_N\_FULL\_CERTIFICATION}; the ceiling is
\texttt{PASS\_A\_TO\_N\_PARTIAL\_CERTIFICATION}, since full certification
requires human review this process cannot self-issue.

\section{Block-by-block results}
\label{sec:blocks}

\subsection{A --- projector validity}
$P=UU^{*}$ is idempotent and self-adjoint to machine precision for any
orthonormal $U$ (5 seeds, plus an exact $n=2$ case at tolerance
$10^{-14}$). This is a \textsc{structural\_identity\_pass} by construction
and is never reported as evidence about the learned subspace's relevance.

\subsection{B --- dynamic commutator explanation}
The candidate explanation $C_\theta = U\Phi U^{*}\Delta K - K\Delta U
\Phi^{*}U^{*}$ for $[\Delta,P] = K\Delta P - P\Delta K$ (exact identity,
both sides rank $\le 2r$) is not capacity-limited in isolation: training
$U$ against \emph{only} the commutator-unexplained loss drives the
residual to $\sim 10^{-9}$ across 5 seeds, matching a closed-form
optimal-$\Phi$ ceiling exactly. Yet in every one of 15 real historical
checkpoints, \texttt{coherence\_ratio} $\le 0$: $C_\theta$ performs at or
worse than predicting zero. A seven-regime ablation matrix isolates the
mechanism (Table~\ref{tab:ablation}): freezing the law and training only
the projector reaches unexplained-rel $0.99$ (barely moves); freezing the
projector and training only the law reaches unexplained-rel exactly $0$
even with $U$ left at its random initial value; training jointly with a
projected-associator objective degrades the commutator residual roughly
$100\times$ relative to isolated training, while a closure objective does
not. The best-supported diagnosis is a genuine, structural conflict
between the commutator-explanation objective and the associator/GJI
family, mediated through the law's parameters --- not insufficient
capacity, not gradient starvation, not the projector search being hard.

\subsection{C --- FINITE\_BEALS\_PROXY}
Renamed to make the scope limit explicit: this block reports finite
nested-commutator Frobenius norms only, never a PsiDO-membership or
microlocal-regularity claim. Adversarially-optimized projectors produce
larger commutator norms (4.35) than random (3.84), smooth (2.85), or
spatially-localized (2.00) hand-picked families, confirming the reported
figures are not favorably cherry-picked.

\subsection{D --- spectral snapping}
Tested on genuine near-projectors $P+\epsilon H$ ($H$ Hermitian), not only
exact ones. Rank recovery and a Davis--Kahan-style bound hold up to the
point the spectral gap closes; an explicit counterexample
($\epsilon=1.2$, gap closes from $1.0$ to $0.51$) reproduces rank
misrecovery, confirming the gap condition is necessary.

\subsection{E --- interscale subspace transport}
Three \emph{independently} initialized and trained resolutions ($n=12,
18, 24$), one frozen Gaussian-kernel lift operator per pair, principal
angles against a random-subspace baseline and an interpolation baseline.
All transported angles sit at $1.41$--$1.53$ rad, essentially the maximum
possible ($\pi/2 = 1.571$); the trained lift beats both baselines in only
2 of 3 pairs, by margins ($0.02$--$0.15$ rad) small relative to how close
every condition already is to full orthogonality. No transport signal is
found, consistent with block F's independent basin-instability finding.

\subsection{F --- rigidity}
The exact Hessian and the generalized Gauss--Newton approximation ($2 J^\top J$)
are computed and never conflated; GGN is confirmed PSD by construction,
the exact Hessian is not (indefinite at a non-minimum, as expected). The
infinitesimal gauge-rotation direction shows Hessian curvature
$-3.5\times10^{-18}$ (numerically flat), confirming identifiability only
modulo gauge --- after a real methodological fix: the first version of
the test loss was not actually gauge-invariant (a single-column
construction, $0.87\%$ discrepancy caught by the test itself), corrected
by summing over all $r$ columns (a Frobenius-norm quantity, exactly
invariant under right-multiplication by a unitary). Basin stability across
3 seeds: all reach near-zero loss, but the pairwise maximum principal
angle between converged subspaces is $1.55$ rad ($\approx 89^\circ$) ---
essentially orthogonal, a genuine non-identifiability finding.

\subsection{G --- n-ary closure}
Upgraded from 8--16 legacy trials to a 2000-sample empirical distribution
plus adversarial gradient ascent (confirmed to find defects at least as
bad as the best random sample). No interval/SOS certified bound was
derived this pass; the claim is \textsc{statistically\_validated\_pass},
not \textsc{pass\_certified}.

\subsection{H --- associator constant}
The triangle-inequality bound $\|A(x,y,z)\| \le 2\hat M^2\|x\|\|y\|\|z\|$
is never violated across 500 trials (300 random + 200 adversarial), but
the maximum observed ratio is $0.452$ --- a $1.55$ gap below the bound.
The constant 2 is empirically far from sharp for this law family (mean
$\cos(T_1,T_2)=0.067$, near-uncorrelated rather than anti-aligned as the
bound's worst case assumes); no analytic tighter constant is derived, so
sharpness remains \textsc{open}.

\subsection{I --- reduced tensor extraction}
Extraction-correctness only, as required. Two independent
implementations (explicit loops; CP-exploiting einsum, including the
cyclic average) agree to $<10^{-10}$ at float64 and $<10^{-4}$ at
float32; an exact rational small case ($n=2$, $r=1$) matches a
hand-expanded computation exactly, with zero floating point.

\subsection{J --- tensor interscale}
The gauge-invariant comparison methodology (raw, Procrustes-aligned,
permutation-aligned, amplitude-ratio, reported separately) is a genuine
improvement over the legacy's single Gram-eigenbasis heuristic. Applied
to block E's real three-resolution experiment: no persistence signal.

\subsection{K --- HOSVD compactness}
A real reduced tensor needs mode ranks $[3,4,4]$ (out of full rank 4) to
reach $99\%$ energy, versus $[4,4,4]$ for a norm-matched random-tensor
null --- modestly, not dramatically, more compact, and only in one of
three modes.

\subsection{L --- gauge canonicalization}
Residual gauge is reported explicitly by spectral-cluster size. A
deliberately constructed degenerate case (eigenvalues $2,1,1$) is
correctly detected, and an \emph{arbitrary} (not infinitesimal) unitary
rotation within the 2-dimensional degenerate eigenspace is confirmed to
produce a pointwise-different but subspace-identical basis (principal
angle $<10^{-8}$ despite Frobenius difference $>0.1$) --- the concrete
demonstration of ``canonical only modulo residual gauge.''

\subsection{M --- persistent factorization}
The comparison tool itself required a real fix during development: an
early version compared HOSVD subspaces via free-unitary Procrustes and
silently reported independent random tensors as gauge-equivalent, because
the unitary group acts transitively on same-size orthonormal frames ---
caught by the block's own required negative control, fixed with principal
angles. Applied to block E's three real, independently-trained
resolutions: rank needed is inconsistent across scales
($\{3,3,4\},\{3,4,4\},\{3,3,3\}$); mean pairwise-mode principal angle
$1.01$ rad; one mode (of nine compared) shows a near-exact match
($1.5\times10^{-8}$ rad) unexplained by this pass and left as an open
anomaly rather than smoothed into a summary verdict.

\subsection{N --- cyclic law and GJI}
Raw (pre-averaging) cyclic defect $4.60$ versus symmetrized (post
cyclic-averaging) defect $8.2\times10^{-33}$ --- a $31$-order-of-magnitude
gap confirming the near-zero symmetrized figure is a construction
identity, never learned evidence. One exact, versioned GJI formula (full
antisymmetrization of the associator over the six permutations of three
arguments) is implemented twice independently and agrees to
$2.8\times10^{-16}$; a deliberate sign-mutation is confirmed detected.
The GJI-ratio supremum is not shown to be bounded (adversarial maximum
$5.98$ against a mean of $0.43$) and is left \textsc{open}.

\section{Fail-closed combination and final state}
\label{sec:final}

No global certificate is computed by averaging. Blocks B, E, J, and M
resolve to \textsc{fail} for their scientific (non-structural) claims;
blocks A, I, L, N carry genuine \textsc{exact}/\textsc{structural\_identity}
components alongside empirical residuals; blocks C, D, G, H, K remain at
\textsc{empirical\_screening\_pass} or weaker, single-seed evidence. The
resulting state is \texttt{FAIL\_CLOSED\_DYNAMIC\_EXPLANATION\_GATE\_NOT\_ESTABLISHED}
(named after the first failing required gate encountered, per
\texttt{evaluate\_global\_certificate}); the honest global label most
consistent with the full evidence set is
\texttt{PASS\_A\_TO\_N\_PARTIAL\_CERTIFICATION at best, pending human review},
never full certification, and several blocks (B, E, J, M) currently sit
in an explicit failed state rather than an unresolved one.

\section{Limitations}
This pass used a closure-only (block E/F/G) or two-term (block B ablation)
training objective for tractability, not the legacy's full $\sim 16$-term
weighted loss; Section~\ref{sec:blocks}'s negative findings for blocks
B/E/J/M are established for the objectives actually tested, not proved to
persist under the historical loss, though block B's own ablation matrix
shows objective choice materially changes outcomes. No independent
literature search was performed; no novelty claim should be read into any
of the methodological corrections described here (Procrustes-on-subspaces,
the gauge-invariant loss fix) beyond ``documented and fixed within this
codebase.'' Track T is entirely out of scope.
