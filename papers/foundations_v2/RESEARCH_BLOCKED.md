# Research v2 release blocker

This manuscript is a rigorous finite-dimensional draft, not a submission-ready
research article.

The exact invariant-reduction theorem and the spectral-snapping estimate are
standard consequences of operadic restriction and Davis--Kahan perturbation
theory. The explicit tree recurrence is proved and experimentally validated,
but the current prior-art review does not establish it as a genuinely new
theorem. The repository therefore refuses to label the foundations paper
release-ready.

Remaining blockers:

1. establish a theorem-level novelty claim that survives primary-source
   comparison, or preserve the work as a technical/preprint draft;
2. obtain verified author email and ORCID metadata;
3. if a future novelty claim is made, add a sharpness/lower-bound or recovery
   guarantee and an independent review;
4. preserve the distinction between operator-norm hypotheses and Frobenius
   upper bounds used in the finite experiments.

The valid outputs are the proof ledger, counterexamples, registered v2 runs,
vector figures, and the draft PDF. The legacy 0.1 paper remains unchanged as a
historical release.
