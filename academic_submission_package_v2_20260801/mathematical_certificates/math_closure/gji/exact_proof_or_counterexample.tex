\section*{M1: Exact symbolic status of the six-term GJI expression}

\subsection*{Object under study}

\texttt{ternary\_declared\_gji} / \texttt{named\_gji\_variants}
(\texttt{src/seion\_core/research\_v3/polynomial\_forests.py}), a six-term
signed combination on 5 leaves $L_0,\dots,L_4$, using the fixed ternary
insertion trees $T_s$ (for slot $s \in \{0,1,2\}$, mu applied to 3
consecutive leaves starting at $s$, nested inside an outer mu on the
remaining 2 leaves plus the inner result):
\[
  T_0 = \mu(\mu(L_0,L_1,L_2), L_3, L_4), \quad
  T_1 = \mu(L_0, \mu(L_1,L_2,L_3), L_4), \quad
  T_2 = \mu(L_0, L_1, \mu(L_2,L_3,L_4)).
\]
The declared six-term expression, with the exact permutations and signs
in the source code, is
\begin{align*}
  \mathrm{GJI}_6 \;=\;
    &+\ \mu(\mu(L_0,L_1,L_2), L_3, L_4) \\
    &+\ \mu(L_1, \mu(L_2,L_0,L_3), L_4) \\
    &+\ \mu(L_2, L_0, \mu(L_1,L_3,L_4)) \\
    &-\ \mu(\mu(L_1,L_0,L_2), L_3, L_4) \\
    &-\ \mu(L_2, \mu(L_1,L_0,L_3), L_4) \\
    &-\ \mu(L_0, L_2, \mu(L_1,L_3,L_4)),
\end{align*}
where $\mu : V^3 \to V$ is contracted positionally,
$\mu(a,b,c)_d = \sum_{i,j,k} \mu_{dijk}\, a_i b_j c_k$, with \emph{no}
symmetry, associativity, or other structure assumed for $\mu$ (matching
\texttt{TypedLaw.apply} / \texttt{np.einsum} exactly, and cross-checked
term-by-term against
\texttt{seion\_core.research\_v3.exact\_evaluation.evaluate\_ambient\_numpy}
on concrete numeric data before any symbolic claim below was trusted).

\subsection*{Prior finding being corrected}

\texttt{docs/research/signed\_forest\_terminal\_status\_v5.md} reported
that this expression evaluates to machine-precision zero across 4{,}000+
random trials plus independent spot checks, ``under both identical and
independently-random leaf inputs,'' and classified it
\texttt{NOT\_CERTIFIABLE\_AS\_DEFINED} pending symbolic verification.

\textbf{Root cause found this session.} The adversarial search
(\texttt{scripts/signed\_forest\_adversarial\_search\_v5.py::forest\_ratio})
always constructs its type system as
\texttt{TypedSpace.coordinate("tau", DIMENSION=2, PROJECTOR\_RANK=1)} and
lifts every leaf's reduced coordinate through this rank-1 embedding. A
rank-1 lift maps every leaf, however its reduced (1-dimensional)
coordinate was drawn, onto a scalar multiple of the \emph{same} fixed
ambient basis vector. So every leaf configuration this search ever tested
-- ``identical'' or ``independently random'' at the reduced-coordinate
level -- was \textbf{collinear} at the ambient level, where the
contraction actually happens. The doc's claim of testing genuinely
independent leaf inputs is true only in the reduced coordinate, not the
ambient vector actually fed to $\mu$.

\subsection*{Result A: not a general identity}

\textbf{Claim tested:} $\mathrm{GJI}_6 \equiv 0$ for every multilinear
$\mu:V^3\to V$ and every 5 (possibly non-collinear) vectors
$L_0,\dots,L_4$.

\textbf{Verdict: \textsc{disproved\_by\_counterexample}.} Two independent
exact methods (Method A: direct symbolic substitution with a fully free
symbolic tensor $\mu$ and free symbolic leaf vectors, dimension $n=2$ and
$n=3$, simplified with \texttt{sympy.expand}; Method B: an independent
monomial-dictionary implementation with no call into sympy's polynomial
engine, tracking exact integer coefficients per
(mu-tensor-entry, leaf-component) monomial) both find $\mathrm{GJI}_6$ is
\emph{not} the zero polynomial in either dimension.

An explicit, minimal, exact-rational counterexample ($n=2$, all data
exact fractions, no floating point):
\[
  \mu_{dijk} \text{ as tabulated in
  \texttt{mutation\_test\_report.json::general\_identity\_claim.exact\_rational\_counterexample}},
  \qquad
  L_0=(1,0),\ L_1=(0,1),\ L_2=(1,1),\ L_3=(2,-1),\ L_4=(-1,3),
\]
gives $\mathrm{GJI}_6 = \left(\tfrac{97}{3}, \tfrac{97}{3}\right) \neq 0$,
verified with exact \texttt{Fraction} arithmetic (reproducible by running
\texttt{scripts/math\_closure\_m1\_gji\_symbolic.py}).

\subsection*{Result B: the true, narrower identity}

\textbf{Claim tested:} $\mathrm{GJI}_6 \equiv 0$ for every multilinear
$\mu:V^3\to V$, whenever all 5 leaves are \emph{collinear}:
$L_i = c_i\, q$ for scalars $c_0,\dots,c_4$ and a single shared vector
$q\in V$.

\textbf{Verdict: \textsc{proved}.} By trilinearity, for any 3 of the 5
leaves fed into an inner application, $\mu(c_a q, c_b q, c_c q) =
c_a c_b c_c\,\mu(q,q,q)$ -- the value depends only on the \emph{product}
of the three scalars (which commute), not on which label carries which
scalar. Composing with the outer application similarly factors out the
remaining two scalars, so \emph{every} term of $\mathrm{GJI}_6$ reduces to
\[
  (c_0 c_1 c_2 c_3 c_4)\ \cdot\ V_s, \qquad
  V_s := (\text{the value of } T_s \text{ with every leaf} = q),
  \quad s\in\{0,1,2\},
\]
where $s$ is determined only by \emph{which outer slot} the inner
application occupies (not by which 3 leaves were grouped into it, nor by
their internal order -- confirmed computationally: a mutation exchanging
two leaf labels within one term, preserving the same partition and slot,
leaves the collinear value unchanged; a mutation changing which slot the
inner application occupies does not). The six terms of $\mathrm{GJI}_6$
occupy slots $(0,1,2,0,1,2)$ with signs $(+,+,+,-,-,-)$ respectively --
i.e.\ exactly the pairing $(+V_0,-V_0), (+V_1,-V_1), (+V_2,-V_2)$ -- so
\[
  \mathrm{GJI}_6 \Big|_{\text{collinear}} =
  (c_0c_1c_2c_3c_4)\big[(V_0 - V_0) + (V_1 - V_1) + (V_2 - V_2)\big] = 0
\]
for \emph{any} $\mu$ and \emph{any} 5 scalars $c_i$ -- confirmed
symbolically (Method A specialized to $L_i = c_i q$, free symbolic $\mu$,
free symbolic $q$ and $c_i$, dimensions $n=2,3$) and by 4 mutation tests
(flip a sign, omit a term, change an insertion slot: all three correctly
produce a nonzero collinear value, confirming the verifier has power;
exchanging two leaf labels within one term does not, which is the exact
invariance predicted by the proof above, not a gap in the verifier).

\subsection*{Why this explains, and does not merely contradict, the prior finding}

The rank-1-projector adversarial search exercised \emph{exactly} the
collinear regime proved above to vanish identically, for 4{,}000 distinct
random laws $\mu$ -- the observed exact zero (to floating-point precision)
in every one of those trials is fully explained by Result B, and is not
evidence for the false general claim the prior doc entertained.

\subsection*{Status}

\texttt{NOT\_CERTIFIABLE\_AS\_DEFINED} (prior) superseded by:
\textsc{disproved\_by\_counterexample} for the general claim, and
\textsc{proved} for the collinear-leaves sub-case, on the explicit
admissible classes stated above. No claim of novelty is made for the
collinear sub-identity pending the originality review (task \#14).
